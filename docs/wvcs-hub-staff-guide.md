# WVCS School Hub Staff Guide

Last updated: August 27, 2026

WVCS School Hub is an official service of Willamette Valley Christian School. The Hub is designed to give staff one place to access school workflows, forms, messages, documents, lunch tools, support requests, and role-specific administrative tools.

This guide is intended for new and existing staff. Some items listed below may not appear for every user because access is based on staff role and assigned permissions.

## 1. Signing In

Use your WVCS email address to sign in to the Hub. If you do not see a module you believe you should have access to, submit a Support Request or contact the office.

Do not share your Hub login link, access email, or signed-in device with another person. The Hub contains school records and student or family information that should only be accessed by authorized users.

## 2. Dashboard

The Dashboard is the main starting point after login. From here, staff can open the modules available to their role.

Common dashboard areas may include:

- Staff Lunch Order
- Structured Recess
- Forms
- Important Documents
- Student Evaluation
- Driver Volunteers
- Support Requests
- Suggestions
- Messaging

Some users may also see restricted tools such as Scheduler, Digital Slips, Office & Finance, Admin, or Staff. These are role-based areas and will not appear for everyone.

## 3. User Profile

Click the profile circle in the top-right corner of the Hub to view your profile.

Your profile shows:

- Signed-in email address
- Hub roles assigned to your account
- Completed staff contracts, when available
- iSolved Timekeeping Resources
- Sign out button

The iSolved Timekeeping Resources button includes staff instructions and resources for:

- Logging in to iSolved
- Using the iSolved mobile app
- Punching in and out
- Submitting a missing punch
- Submitting a time off request
- Viewing time off balances
- Accessing iSolved training materials

## 4. Messaging

The Hub messaging system allows staff to send and receive messages within the Hub. When a message is sent, the recipient may receive an email notification letting them know they have a Hub message.

Best practices:

- Use Hub messaging for school-related communication that should stay organized in one place.
- Reply inside the Hub when possible so the conversation stays together.
- Keep messages professional and limited to appropriate school business.
- Do not include unnecessary sensitive details if a brief message will accomplish the purpose.

## 5. Support Requests

Use Support Requests for IT, maintenance, facilities, supplies, and operations needs.

When submitting a request:

- Choose the correct request type.
- Give a clear title.
- Include enough detail for the support team to understand the issue.
- Add location, device, room number, or urgency when helpful.
- Attach screenshots or files when they would help explain the issue.

After submitting, the request can be reviewed by the appropriate support team. Replies to support emails should go back to the person who submitted the request.

## 6. Forms

The Forms module is used for staff forms that need to be submitted, reviewed, approved, or routed to the correct person.

Examples may include:

- Facilities use requests
- Supply order forms
- General staff forms
- Other school forms added by administration

When completing forms:

- Fill in all required fields.
- Use the requested format for phone numbers, dates, emails, or other structured fields.
- Review your form before submitting.
- Watch for validation messages if something is missing or formatted incorrectly.

If a form needs approval, the appropriate approver will receive the submission.

## 7. Important Documents

Important Documents is a staff resource area for documents the school wants staff to access from the Hub.

Documents may be downloadable, viewable, or editable depending on how they were uploaded by administration.

Use this area for current school documents rather than relying on old copies saved elsewhere.

## 8. Structured Recess

Structured Recess is used to manage and view students assigned to structured recess.

Staff may use this area to:

- Add students for upcoming days, if permitted
- View students assigned for the day
- Use the Aide View for a simplified live board
- Track notes or status connected to structured recess

Only enter information that is necessary for staff supervision and school operations.

## 9. Lunch Tools

The Hub includes lunch tools for staff and office workflows.

Staff Lunch Order:

- Staff may open the published lunch menu and submit lunch choices.
- Staff lunch is recorded separately from student lunch billing.
- Submitted orders can be reviewed or edited when the menu allows.

Daily Lunch Log:

- Aides or approved staff may open the daily lunch log.
- Students can be marked served, absent, canceled, or manually added when needed.
- This helps the office charge lunch accounts accurately.

Family lunch balances and payments are handled through the family portal and Office & Finance tools.

## 10. Driver Volunteers

The Driver Volunteers module lists parent drivers who are verified to drive.

Staff can use this area to confirm whether a parent is currently verified. Grade filtering may be available to help identify drivers connected to students in a particular grade.

Driver verification is managed through office workflows and family forms. Staff should not assume a parent is cleared to drive unless the Hub shows current verification.

## 11. Student Evaluation

Student Evaluation is used for tracking probation or evaluation windows for new students.

Use this area only for appropriate school evaluation information. Keep notes professional, factual, and limited to what is needed for school follow-up.

## 12. Meeting Requests

Meeting Requests can be used to request meetings with administrators or school leaders.

Include:

- Reason for the meeting
- Preferred time or urgency
- Relevant background information

Keep meeting requests concise but clear enough for the recipient to respond appropriately.

## 13. Suggestions

Suggestions is a staff suggestion box for ideas, needs, improvements, or process feedback.

This area is useful for:

- Ideas to improve school operations
- Requests for consideration
- Workflow improvements
- Feedback about the Hub

## 14. Digital Permission Slips

Digital Slips is a restricted module used to create and send digital permission slips to families.

Authorized users can:

- Create permission slip templates
- Send permission slips to families
- Track who has signed
- View signed PDFs
- Export signed lists
- Download signed permission slips

This area uses student roster data and parent contact information. Because it involves student and family records, only authorized users should use it.

## 15. Office & Finance

Office & Finance is restricted to users with office or finance access.

This area may include:

- Incidentals
- Family Records
- Accounts Receivable
- Family Ledger
- Lunch administration
- FOS tracking
- Substitutes
- Tuition Breakdowns
- Settings

Office & Finance contains sensitive financial and family information. Staff should only access this area if it is part of their role.

Key reminders for authorized users:

- Record payments accurately by date received.
- Categorize payments correctly.
- Use family records to keep household information connected.
- Use accounts receivable for money received through the Hub or office, except regular monthly tuition processed through FACTS.
- Keep notes factual and professional.

## 16. Family Portal

The Family Portal is separate from the staff Hub. Families use it to view family-facing information and complete family workflows.

Families may use the portal for:

- FOS balance and volunteer hour submissions
- Lunch menu orders
- Lunch account funds
- Invoice history
- Family forms
- Permission slip access, where applicable

Staff should remember that parents do not have access to the staff Hub unless they are also authorized staff users.

## 17. Scheduler

Scheduler is a restricted tool used for building and managing school schedules.

Authorized users may use it for:

- Master schedule planning
- Teacher schedules
- Elementary pull-out schedules
- Saved schedule versions
- Printable schedule copies

Only users assigned the scheduler role should see this area.

## 18. Staff Area

The Staff area is restricted and may include tools such as:

- Staff contracts
- Staff payroll worksheet
- Curriculum tools

Some staff may only see completed contracts in their user profile, while administrative staff may have access to contract or payroll management tools.

## 19. Admin Area

The Admin area is restricted to users with administrative permissions.

Depending on assigned access, it may include:

- User and access controls
- Module administration
- Forms administration
- Documents administration
- Support request administration
- Email audit
- Google Drive backup settings
- Student directory tools
- Other Hub settings

Adding users, assigning roles, and managing sensitive settings should only be done by authorized superusers.

## 20. Good Hub Habits

Use the Hub as the official place for workflows it supports.

Best practices:

- Sign out when using a shared or public computer.
- Do not use another staff member's account.
- Check recipients carefully before sending messages, forms, invoices, or invites.
- Keep student and family information confidential.
- Use clear notes that another staff member could understand later.
- Avoid storing sensitive information outside the Hub when the Hub already tracks it.
- Submit a Support Request when something does not work as expected.

## 21. What To Do If Something Looks Wrong

If you notice missing information, duplicate records, incorrect family information, wrong permissions, or a feature that does not respond:

1. Submit a Support Request.
2. Include the student's name, family name, form, invoice, or module involved.
3. Include what you expected to happen.
4. Include what actually happened.
5. Add a screenshot when helpful.

This helps the issue get diagnosed faster.

## 22. Quick Start For New Staff

On your first day using the Hub:

1. Sign in with your WVCS email.
2. Open your profile in the top-right corner and confirm your email and roles.
3. Open iSolved Timekeeping Resources from your profile.
4. Review Important Documents.
5. Submit a test Support Request only if directed by administration.
6. Explore the modules available to your role.
7. Ask the office if you believe a needed module is missing.

## 23. Privacy Reminder

The Hub contains school operational records and may include student, family, staff, financial, and form information. Treat Hub access the same way you would treat confidential school files.

Only access information you need for your role, and only use it for school purposes.
